# reference: a site the owner admires
Its structure and look, never its words or pictures: a few pages, their screenshots, the header and footer as structure, the styles.

## Run
```
tt-crawl reference https://site-they-like.com
```
Eight pages, one of each kind, no pictures fetched, into `raw/external/<host>/`.

## Use
Borrow the composition, rhythm and feel from `shots/`, `_index/furniture.json` and `_index/styles.json`. Never their copy, images, logo or name: they are someone else's, and nothing in `raw/external/` is ever the owner's fact.
