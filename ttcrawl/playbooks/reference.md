# reference: a site the owner admires
Its structure and look, never its words or pictures: a few pages, their screenshots, the nav and footer as structure, the styles.

## Run
```
tt-crawl reference https://site-they-like.com
```
`reference` is `site --external --images none --screenshots --styles --max-pages 8 --per-template 1`, into `raw/external/<host>/`.

## Use
Borrow the composition, rhythm and feel from `shots/`, `_index/furniture.json` and `_index/styles.json`. Never their copy, images, logo or name: those are someone else's, and nothing in `raw/external/` is ever the owner's fact.
